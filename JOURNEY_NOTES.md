# PCB&T homepage journey / V11

**Understand:** Hero offers a clear proposition and a single primary CTA.

**Explore:** Four most relevant services are visible immediately after the hero; all remaining services and industries retain dedicated pages through links.

**Trust the process:** Three brief steps explain the working relationship, without fabricated reviews or statistics.

**Act:** One closing conversation CTA, followed by a deliberately small footer.

Removed from the homepage: three large benefit cards, long before/after comparison, coffee message block, full industries directory, duplicate process illustration, redundant editorial reassurance section and oversized closing footer headline. These ideas remain accessible via Services, Industries, Our Approach, and About.

Header is transparent above every hero until scrolling; the desktop mega menu itself uses a translucent frosted backdrop for legibility. The scrolled header is light and readable, and hides when scrolling down / reappears scrolling up.
