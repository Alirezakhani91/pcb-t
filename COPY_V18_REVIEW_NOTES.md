# PCB&T — Professional Content Edition V18

Updated all 25 English-language pages while keeping the established V17 branding, original logos, responsive design and animations.

## Copy approach
- Speak to a business owner's real problem, then explain the accounting work and the practical outcome.
- Differentiate bookkeeping, payroll, tax preparation support, financial reporting, budgeting and specialized accounting.
- Make industry pages operationally relevant: POS settlement for restaurants, job costs for construction, property-level income and expenses for rentals, and grant/program categories where applicable for nonprofits.
- Describe deliverables in conditional language: scope, frequency and particular regulatory requirements are defined by the client engagement.
- Never claim the company holds a CPA designation, is an auditor, guarantees tax outcomes, has a defined client count, or automatically integrates with named software.
- Educational article pages now contain substantial practical guidance and Canadian CRA references.
