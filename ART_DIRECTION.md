# V14 Art Direction — PCB&T

The original PCB&T logo is preserved byte-for-byte in `assets/pcbt-logo.png`. The source was already transparent. The decorative gold ray mask is extracted from the original yellow pixels without redrawing the artwork and is never used as a substitute for the actual logo.

**Visual system:** Editorial photography, Times New Roman, sea-glass teal, deep navy, sun-gold hairlines, and original rays used as faint atmospheric accents.

**Motion system:** Scroll-triggered horizon drawing in hero and CTA, subtle abstract wayfinding curves through the process, gentle sun-ray atmospheric drift, subtle cursor-following light, responsive service hover hairlines, and the existing premium section reveal. Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

**Customer journey:** Understand the offer → identify the right service → understand the collaborative process → contact.

**Scope:** 25 English HTML pages, local photographs and images, editable CSS and vanilla JavaScript. The site remains static and suitable for static hosts such as GitHub Pages once the contact and legal items are reviewed.
