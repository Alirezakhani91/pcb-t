# Image provenance / Image assets (V10)

Twenty-five different hero scenes are bundled as compressed WebP files under assets/hero, one per HTML page. No hero image is repeated on another page. Four are newly composed still-life visualizations for financial reporting, forecasting, tax support, and bookkeeping. Hero images are illustrative synthetic photography rather than documentary photographs of PCB&T employees or real clients.

All production image URLs are relative (assets/...), do not require third-party image hosts, and can be used in a static deployment. If strictly authentic, non-AI photography is required, replace the hero pictures with properly licensed photography before launch.
